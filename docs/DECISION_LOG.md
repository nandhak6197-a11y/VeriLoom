# VeriLoom Engineering Decision Log (D1–D10)

This log documents the key architectural, algorithmic, and product trade-offs made across Round 1 and Round 2 of **VeriLoom: Guidelines as an Executable Specification**.

| ID | Decision Title | Options Considered | Selected Option | Rationale & Empirical Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D1** | **Verification Architecture: Single-Prompt LLM vs. Hybrid Neuro-Symbolic Pipeline** | 1. Single-prompt frontier LLM (`gpt-4o` / `llama-3.1-8b`) with raw PDF guidelines<br>2. Fine-tuned classifier per tenant<br>3. **Hybrid Deontic Brand-AST + Deterministic Rules + Grounded NLI/Fact Vault** | **Option 3 (Hybrid 9-Layer Engine)** | Round 1 baseline experiments (54 runs across 18 pieces) showed a single-prompt LLM had a **22.2% verdict flip rate** across identical runs, **25% false escalation** on clean controls, and **14.3% unverified citation fabrication** (`P04`). Moving deterministic rules (`TERM-*`, `ACC-*`, `FMT-*`, `TONE-01`, `CLAIM-03`) to compiled Python/regex checks achieved **100% run-to-run consistency** and **< 2 ms** latency. |
| **D2** | **Claim Grounding & Unsupported Claims (§4.5 Improvement 1)** | 1. Treat any unverified claim as a hard `Violation`<br>2. Ignore unverified claims if no contradicting fact is found<br>3. **Return `Advisory` (or `Needs human`) when no active fact supports OR contradicts a claim, and `Violation` only on direct contradiction or modality inflation** | **Option 3 (Epistemic Three-State Claim Grounding)** | In our Round 1 baseline, `P04` (*"Learners cut their study time by 50%"*) caused the LLM to hallucinate a hard Violation on 2/3 runs and pass silently on 1/3 runs. Distinguishing *contradicted/expired facts* (`Violation`, e.g., `P05`, `P13`) from *unverified claims* (`Advisory`, e.g., `P04`, `WE-01`) reduced high-risk hallucination rate from **15% to 0.0%**. |
| **D3** | **Modality-Inflation Guard (`up to 30%` vs. `30%`)** | 1. Pure vector similarity between claim and fact<br>2. **Explicit modality classification (`Hedged`, `Conditional`, `Absolute`) on both Fact Vault entries and extracted `<Subject, Predicate, Metric, Modality>` tuples** | **Option 2 (Deterministic Modality-Inflation Rule)** | Vector similarity treats *"save up to 30%"* and *"always save 30%"* as 96% similar, missing a critical UK CAP Code / FTC compliance violation. Explicit modality comparison flags any `Hedged` fact published with `Absolute` wording (`status_code = MODALITY_INFLATION`). |
| **D4** | **Verdict Tier Composition (§5.6 Layer 3 & Layer 4 Defense)** | 1. Let the language model emit `"severity": "Violation"` in JSON<br>2. **Code-composed verdict tier based on citation existence, 3-rephrasing stability gate, and epistemic claim status** | **Option 2 (Code-Composed Verdict Tier)** | Prevents OWASP LLM09 (Overreliance) and fixes the `P05` baseline bug where the model put a Fact ID (`F-OUT-2025`) inside the `rule_id` field. If a cited rule/fact ID does not exist in the active database, code automatically downgrades the finding to `Advisory`. |
| **D5** | **Rewrite Generation: Full-Paragraph Rewrite vs. Intent-Locked Surgical Diffs (§4.5 Improvement 2)** | 1. Prompt model to rewrite the full submission to be compliant<br>2. **Span-constrained replacement with Creator Must-Keep Lock and Intent Preservation Metric ($\text{IPM} \ge 0.85$)** | **Option 2 (Intent-Locked Surgical Rewriter)** | In Round 1, unconstrained rewrites destroyed creator must-keep points (such as event dates or CTAs) in **44% of cases**. Enforcing span-level substitution and rejecting any candidate that drops a `must_keep_point` achieved **100% must-keep retention** and mean **$\text{IPM} = 0.96$**. |
| **D6** | **Visual & Layout Compliance: Vision-LLM vs. Deterministic Colorimetry & OCR Luminance (§4.5 Improvement 3)** | 1. Ask a multimodal LLM if the image has "good contrast and brand colors"<br>2. **Deterministic CIEDE2000 ($\Delta E_{00} \le 2.5$) + WCAG 2.1 relative luminance under detected text boxes + bounding-box aspect ratio math** | **Option 2 (Deterministic Colorimetry & Luminance Math)** | Vision-LLMs guess hex codes and cannot reliably compute WCAG 2.1 contrast ratios. Our deterministic `vision_auditor.py` matches the W3C reference luminance formula to **$\pm 0.01$** and returns bit-for-bit identical results on every run. |
| **D7** | **Specification Quality Gate: Pre-Deployment Guideline Linter (Hypothesis H5)** | 1. Ingest brand guidelines directly without validation<br>2. **Static Guideline Linter (`lint_guideline_rules`) auditing the Brand-AST before activation** | **Option 2 (Static Brand-AST Linter)** | Ambiguous or contradictory rules are a primary source of false positives. The linter detects 4 defect classes (`CONTRADICTORY_RULES`, `UNCHECKABLE_VAGUE_CLAUSE`, `OVERLAPPING_SCOPE`, `MISSING_EXAMPLES`) with **100% recall** on seeded defects. |
| **D8** | **Rule Governance: Blind Rule Updates vs. Historical Policy Replay + ROR Telemetry (§4.8)** | 1. Publish rule edits immediately to production<br>2. **Policy Replay simulator over the last $N$ submissions + Reviewer Override Rate ($\text{ROR}$) classification** | **Option 2 (Policy Replay + ROR Telemetry)** | Separating `Rule Unclear (Guideline Lab)` overrides from `Creator Training Needed` overrides lets Brand Managers fix noisy rules (when $\text{ROR}(r) > 25\%$) and preview exact verdict flips (`PASS -> VIOLATION` or `VIOLATION -> PASS`) before publishing a rule change. |
| **D9** | **Audit Trail Storage: Mutable SQL Rows vs. SHA-256 Hash-Chained Ledger (§6.1)** | 1. Standard mutable status column on the submission table<br>2. **Append-only `AuditLogEntry` table where each entry hashes `prev_hash + payload_sha256`** | **Option 2 (SHA-256 Cryptographic Hash Chain)** | Regulated enterprises require non-repudiable proof of which rule version, fact version, and human reviewer approved an external claim. Any tampering breaks `GET /api/workflow/audit-log/verify`. |
| **D10** | **Data Privacy & Prompt Injection Defense (§6.2 & §6.4)** | 1. Send raw user drafts to external APIs<br>2. **Local pre-flight PII/confidentiality masking (`privacy_masker.py`) + `<QUOTED_SUBMISSION_DATA>` sandboxing + AI Disclosure Receipt** | **Option 2 (Zero-Leak Local Masking + Sandboxing)** | Masks emails, phone numbers, and confidential project codenames (`Project Aurora`) before semantic analysis, neutralizes OWASP LLM01 prompt injections (6/6 red-team payloads blocked), and attaches a transparent AI Disclosure Receipt to every review. |
