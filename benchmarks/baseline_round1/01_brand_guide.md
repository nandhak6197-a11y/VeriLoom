# Northwind Academy: brand guide (for review)

Use only this guide. Check the content against every rule. Do not use outside knowledge of the brand.

## Terminology
- TERM-01: Write "learners" in external copy. Do not use "students".
- TERM-02: Do not use "cheap". Use "affordable".
- TERM-03: The product name is "Northwind Academy" (or "Northwind" on second mention). "NW Academy" is not permitted.

## Tone
- TONE-01: No hype words: "revolutionary", "best-ever", "game-changing".
- TONE-02: Do not use fear or job-loss pressure to sell courses.
- TONE-03: Do not shame learners for their choices or circumstances.

## Claims
- CLAIM-01: Quantified claims about results, time savings or performance need a matching approved fact in the Fact Ledger below.
- CLAIM-02: Outcome statistics (jobs, promotions, salary) must match the current approved fact. Contradicting an approved fact is a violation.
- CLAIM-03: Do not promise or guarantee outcomes, jobs or results.
- FACT-01: Durations, prices and counts must match a current approved fact. Expired facts are stale.

## Accessibility
- ACC-01: Every image has alt text that describes its content. Content given as "Alt text: none" breaches this rule.
- ACC-02: Text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background. A value below 4.5:1 breaches this rule.
- ACC-03: Link text describes the destination. Do not use "click here".
- ACC-04: Video has captions. Content given as "Captions: none" breaches this rule.

## Format
- FMT-01: Dates are written day month year, for example 30 November 2026.

## Legal
- LEGAL-01: No medical or health claims. Courses do not treat, cure or prevent any condition.

## Approved facts (Fact Ledger)
- F-OUT-2025: 71% of 2025 completers reported a new role or promotion within six months (Outcomes Report 2025). Valid to 31 December 2026.
- F-DUR-01: Data Foundations is 12 weeks. EXPIRED 30 June 2026. Do not use.
- F-DUR-02: Data Foundations is 10 weeks. Effective 1 July 2026. Valid to 30 June 2027.
- F-PRICE-01: Monthly plans start at £15. Valid to 31 March 2027.
- F-LEARN-01: Over 40,000 learners since 2019. Valid to 31 December 2027.
