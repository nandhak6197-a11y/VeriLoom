# Observation log: public practitioner discussions (§3.4B)

Read the threads yourself. Record only what you saw. Aim for 6 to 10 items. Do not record usernames, profile links or personal details. Use the platform's own search, filter to the last 12 months, and note the date you searched.

## Search queries to start from
Reddit: "brand guidelines" AI review; "brand guide" approval slow; "brand consistency" agency; "content approval" marketing team; AI ad backlash marketing
Quora: How do companies enforce brand guidelines; How do marketing teams approve content; AI brand compliance tools
LinkedIn: "brand guidelines" approval process; "content governance" marketing; "brand compliance" AI
X: "brand guidelines" AI wrong; brand "not following" guidelines; approved then changed brand ad

## Codebook (choose one code per item)
- C1: Unclear or conflicting rules
- C2: Approval delays or unclear approvers
- C3: AI-generated output errors
- C4: Trust in automated checks
- C5: Disagreement with a reviewer
- C6: Workflow and file-handling problems
